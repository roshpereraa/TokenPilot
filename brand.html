PilotPad's identity is built on one idea: a guiding star with a token in orbit around it. Calm, precise and quietly luminous — the confidence of a pilot who knows the route.

Logo

The Polaris mark

A four-point star — the pilot's fixed point — encircled by a tilted orbit that passes behind and in front of it. The orbit is the token; the star is the strategy that keeps it on course. The gradient runs from Indigo to Signal: from idea to growth.

Material

Liquid glass

Surfaces are panes of glass floating over deep space: a refractive bezel, a soft specular highlight along the top edge and a gradient hairline. Glass is for structure; content always stays crisp.

Refraction

In supporting browsers, the bezel of each pane bends what's behind it, like a real lens. Everywhere else it degrades to frosted glass.

Liquid glass

Motion

Motion is slow and purposeful: elements rise and come into focus as they enter, the command center tilts up to meet you, and agents breathe with a quiet pulse. Everything respects reduced-motion settings.
