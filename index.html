Open the app, brief your token and start using every agent straight away. No sign-up and no card. The trial runs for 14 days from your first brief. After that, choose monthly or annual billing to keep going.

$99/month in your first year, $120/month in your second and $199/month from your third year on. Pay annually and save 30%: $831.60, $1,008 and $1,671.60 a year.

Does PilotPad deploy or trade my token?

No. PilotPad is a strategy, brand and growth system. It never holds keys, deploys contracts or touches funds. Nothing it produces is financial advice.

Why Robinhood Chain?

PilotPad is tuned for founders launching on Robinhood Chain: its retail-first audience, its ecosystem and its launch dynamics. The playbooks adapt to wherever your community lives.

Will the content sound generic?

Every agent shares one memory of your project — your narrative, voice and audience — and you can edit, approve and train it as you go. The more you use it, the more it sounds like you.

What happens after launch?

The work continues. Log performance and the system adapts: new priorities each day, updated campaigns, and early warnings when narratives or sentiment shift.
